## When to use this tool

Math Grading helps you **mark handwritten math work with structured feedback**. Use it when you have many worked solutions to assess and want to weigh the reasoning, not just the final answer.

## How it works

It's a **one-shot generator with image input**. Upload a photo of the handwritten response (PNG or JPEG), give your learning objectives and your rubric; you get a total score, strengths, areas for improvement, detailed feedback, and next steps.

## Tips

- **Take a sharp, straight photo** with good lighting — scan quality drives assessment quality.
- **Make your rubric explicit with points** (e.g. correct answer 5pt, steps 3pt, notation 2pt) for a consistent score.
- **Check the handwriting transcription**: verify any doubtful digits or symbols yourself.
- **Use it as an aid, not a final verdict** — you remain responsible for the grade.
- Tie in your [context profile](/help/context-profiles) so the feedback fits your subject and level.

> Source: the *Grading Handwritten Math Responses* chapter of *The Pedagogical Promptbook* (effective feedback, Hattie & Timperley; Shute).
