## When do you use this tool?

Your students held an oral defense with their own AI, using the text from **Defend your own work out loud**, and handed in the conversation. With this tool you review such a conversation. You get a draft: how well the student understands their own work, which learner attributes stood out, feedback you can share with the student, and notes for yourself.

## How does it work?

1. **Paste the conversation.** Preferably put [AI] before the expert's messages and [Student] before the student's.
2. **Choose the kind of work** and fill in the subject or programme and the learning objective. If you have a course, you can take over its learning outcomes. You may add your own criteria for the four levels.
3. **Anonymise first.** Before sending, your browser replaces names, the student number, the company and supervisors with codes. You check the codes and add what was missed. Only the codes go to the AI; the result shows the names again.
4. **Read the draft** and decide yourself.

## What the tool does and doesn't do

- **Does:** one of four levels (exemplary, proficient, developing, beginning), with moments and short quotes from the conversation.
- **Does:** look only at what the student said. If the AI gave away an answer, the notes for you say so.
- **Doesn't:** give a grade or "passed". It is a draft; you assess.
- **Honest limit:** anonymising catches names and numbers, but a recognisable description can slip through. Check the text yourself before sending.
