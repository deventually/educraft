## When to use this tool

Cognitive Architect builds a **coherent learning unit in stages** (inspired by Gagné's nine events of instruction): from design to learning activities, assessment, and feedback. Use it when you don't want a single isolated artifact but a deliberate, aligned sequence.

## How it works

This is a **multi-stage tool**. Each stage builds on the previous one: the output of stage 1 feeds stage 2, and so on. You work through the steps one at a time.

1. Complete stage 1 (the design) carefully — everything after builds on it.
2. Only generate the next stage once the previous one is right; you can regenerate a stage.
3. Read the final result as a whole: does the assessment match the activities and the goals?

## Tips

- **Invest in stage 1.** A sharp design makes every later stage better.
- **Set a rich [context profile](/help/context-profielen)** — with multi-stage output, context counts double.
- **Unhappy with a stage?** Regenerate *that* stage before moving on.
