## When to use this tool

Bloom by Design helps you **choose educational technology based on learning outcomes**, not on what's popular or new. Use it when you're unsure which tool, activity, or platform fits what students need to be able to do — and you want that choice to be pedagogically justified (constructive alignment).

## How it works

It's a **conversation**. You start with your learning outcome; the tool asks follow-up questions and aligns activities, assessment, and technology.

1. **Learning outcome** — state what students should be able to do, ideally measurable (e.g. "analyse case studies").
2. **How will you measure success?** — name the cognitive level (Bloom), e.g. Analysis (L4).
3. **Teaching context** — subject area, group size, delivery (online/hybrid).

## Tips

- **Start from the outcome, not the tool.** The chatbot deliberately reverses the reasoning: technology follows from goals.
- **Be specific about the Bloom level** — it drives which activities fit (remembering ≠ creating).
- **Ask for the *why*** behind a recommendation; the tool explains the pedagogical reasoning.
