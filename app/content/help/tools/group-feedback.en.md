## When do you use this tool?

Use this tool **after marking a pile of work**, when you keep seeing the same mistakes. You make one debrief for the whole group instead of repeating everything per student.

## How does it work?

1. **Open the tool from your rubric.** On the rubric page, under *How the group did*, the button *Make group feedback* fills in the counts of the chosen round. You can also paste the results of the test analysis.
2. **Add example mistakes if you like**, briefly. **No names:** names are replaced in your browser before anything is sent.
3. You get three to five points, each with how many pieces of work it concerned, what went wrong, a made-up example of good work and a ten-minute exercise. Below them is a short text to share with the group.

## What the tool does and does not do

- **Does:** copy the counts from your rubric or test analysis literally. LimeOnIt counts, the AI does not. LimeOnIt removes a number that is not in your input.
- **Does not:** use a student's work as an example. The example of good work is made up.
- **Does not:** judge persons. The debrief is about the work of the group.

## Tips

- The counts only appear from five closed fill-ins in a round, so no student can be recognised.
- Export the debrief to Word to adapt or share it.

> Original LimeOnIt instrument, after the feedback model of Hattie and Timperley (2007).
