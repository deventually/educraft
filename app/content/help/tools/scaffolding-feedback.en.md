## When to use this tool

Scaffolding Feedback is a **patient tutor who walks you through a problem step by step**, with warm feedback. Use it when math stresses you out or when you'd rather work in small, manageable steps.

## How it works

It's a **chat tool**. Give the subject and a bit about your background; the tutor builds difficulty slowly and lets support fade as you can do more yourself. Start with a suggestion or type your own message. You can stop or regenerate a reply.

## Tips

- **Be honest about your background** ("I have math anxiety") — the tutor adapts its tone and pace.
- **Do the steps yourself**: the tutor deliberately waits so you make the thinking move.
- **Stuck? Just say so** — you get a smaller intermediate step, not a judgement.
- **Celebrate small wins**; building confidence is part of the learning here.

> Source: the *Scaffolding with Formative Feedback* chapter of *The Pedagogical Promptbook* (scaffolding, Vygotsky; Sweller; Hattie & Timperley).
