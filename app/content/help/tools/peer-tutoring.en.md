## When to use this tool

Peer Tutoring helps you **critically evaluate research sources** with an equal peer alongside you. Use it for a literature review or paper when you want to learn whether a source is reliable, relevant, and peer-reviewed.

## How it works

It's a **chat tool**. Give your assignment, discipline, and level; your peer guides you step by step through source evaluation using frameworks like CRAAP, RADAR, and SIFT. Start with a suggestion or type your own question. You can stop or regenerate.

## Tips

- **Bring a concrete source** (title, author, year, where you found it) — it makes the evaluation sharp.
- **Learn the framework, not just the verdict**: ask *why* a source scores strong or weak.
- **Unsure about peer review?** Ask the tutor how to verify it yourself.
- It's a peer, not an oracle — use it to train your own judgement.

> Source: the *Peer Tutoring* chapter of *The Pedagogical Promptbook* (critical thinking, Kuhn; Metzger & Flanagin).
