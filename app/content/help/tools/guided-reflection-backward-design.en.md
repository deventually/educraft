## When to use this tool

Guided Reflection helps you **design reflection tasks using Backward Design**: you start from what students should learn *from* the reflection and work back to the questions and prompt that elicit it. Use it for internships, projects, portfolios, or professional products where you want more than "what went well/badly".

## How it works

It's a **one-shot generator**: fill in the fields and generate in one pass. You get a worked-out reflection task with goals, reflection questions, and (where appropriate) a way to assess it.

## Tips

- **Name the learning outcome of the reflection itself**, not just the topic. Reflection is a learning activity, not a report.
- **Tie it to the professional field** via your [context profile](/help/context-profiles) — the questions become more concrete.
- **Iterate**: tweak a field and regenerate if the depth or tone isn't right yet.
- Output too generic? Make the learning goal and context more specific.

> Source: the *Guided Reflection* chapter of *The Pedagogical Promptbook* (Backward Design, Wiggins & McTighe).
