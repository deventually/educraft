## When to use this tool

Think-Pair-Share gives you **an equal peer to sharpen your thinking with**. Use it when you want to think a question or claim through more thoroughly: first think alone, then dialogue, then summarise.

## How it works

It's a **chat tool** with three phases — Think, Pair, Share. Give your discipline and a guiding question; your peer asks questions, brings in alternative perspectives, and helps you toward a synthesis. Start with a suggestion or type your own message. You can stop or regenerate.

## Tips

- **Share your own thought first** (the "Think") before letting the peer respond — that's the core of the method.
- **Stay open to pushback**: the peer deliberately brings in other angles.
- **Work toward a synthesis** ("Share"): what's your position now, and why?
- Phrase an open guiding question; yes/no questions yield little dialogue.
