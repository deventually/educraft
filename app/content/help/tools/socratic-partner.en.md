## When to use this tool

Socratic Partner tests and deepens **your understanding through questions, not answers**. Use it to check whether you really get a chapter: you explain it and the "author" asks questions that expose gaps.

## How it works

It's a **chat tool**. Give the subject and the chapter title; an imagined author of your learning material probes your reasoning. Start with a suggestion or type your own message. You can stop or regenerate a reply.

## Tips

- **Try to explain out loud**, even incompletely — the questions work precisely on what you haven't sharpened yet.
- **Don't give up too fast on a hard question**: the "being stuck" is where the learning happens.
- **Summarise in your own words** at the end; notice whether you can retell it.
- Don't expect a ready-made answer — the tool reflects your understanding back to you.

> Source: the *From Oracle to Socratic Partner* chapter of *The Pedagogical Promptbook* (Socratic dialogue, Paul; Chin & Brown).
