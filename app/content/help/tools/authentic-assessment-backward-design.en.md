## When to use this tool

Authentic Assessment helps you **design a realistic professional task instead of an exam for the exam's sake**. Use it when you want students to demonstrate what they can do in a practice-like context — with a rubric and feedback that scale.

## How it works

It's a **one-shot generator** that follows Backward Design: from learning outcomes and "acceptable evidence" to the task and the assessment. You get a full Assessment Blueprint: the task, an analytic rubric, a student-facing rubric, a feedback starter, and attention to UDL and assessment integrity.

## Tips

- **Start from the outcome and the evidence**, not the task format — that's the heart of Backward Design.
- **Make the authentic role concrete**: who is the student (junior developer, consultant…), who do they deliver to, and what?
- **Be honest about your constraints** (grading capacity, accreditation) so the assessment stays feasible.
- **Set the iteration level realistically**; draft + feedback needs different planning than a single submission.
- Working across a programme? Tie in your [context profile](/help/context-profielen) for aligned competencies.
