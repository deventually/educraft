## When to use this tool

MentorAI is your **personal mentor**: instead of ready-made answers, the mentor asks questions that get your own thinking going. Use it when you genuinely want to understand something, not just copy the answer.

## How it works

It's a **chat tool** based on cognitive apprenticeship (modeling, coaching, scaffolding). Enter your discipline, level, and learning goal; the mentor guides you step by step. Start with a suggestion or type your own. You can stop or regenerate a reply.

## Tips

- **Think aloud**: share your reasoning, even when unsure — that's what the mentor builds on.
- **Be specific about your learning goal** ("I want to understand how OOP works") for focused guidance.
- **Don't let it hand you the answer too soon**: the value is in taking the step yourself.
- **Ask for an example** when you're stuck; the mentor can model it and then hand it back to you.

> Source: the *From Model to Mentor* chapter of *The Pedagogical Promptbook* (cognitive apprenticeship, Collins, Brown & Newman).
