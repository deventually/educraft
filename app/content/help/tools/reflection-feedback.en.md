## What it is for

You coach students and want to help one forward with a reflection report or personal development plan. This tool lays the piece next to an assessment model and gives, per criterion, **what is already strong** (with a quote from the piece) and **the next step**. It never gives a grade or level: this is feedforward, not an assessment.

## How it works

1. Choose the **kind of piece**: reflection report or development plan.
2. Upload or paste the piece. **No names or personal details:** before anything leaves, your browser shows which names are still in it and you replace them with codes.
3. Pick an **assessment model**. For a reflection report there is a starter: Korthagen's reflection cycle (ALACT), with its source. For a development plan, make your own model under *Assessment models*.
4. Generate, and discuss the feedback with the student.

## What happens to the text

- The feedback is **not kept**. Copy it or download it as Word.
- LimeOnIt itself removes a line with a grade or level, even when the AI writes one anyway.
- When a reflection is about health, home or money, the tool gives no advice on it. Discuss that with the student yourself, and refer them on when needed.
