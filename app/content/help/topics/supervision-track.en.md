# Internship and thesis track

Under **My supervision** you keep a track for each student you supervise during an internship or a graduation project. The track runs alongside OnStage: LimeOnIt helps you give and keep feedback, and OnStage stays the place of the official file and the assessment you sign.

## Opening a track

1. Go to **My supervision** (on Start, or via *All tracks*).
2. Enter a **code**, for example the number from your own list. Use no name and no student number.
3. Choose **Internship** or **Thesis**. If you like, link a course and an assessment model. The model gives the criteria, in the order of the OnStage form.

## The milestones

A track has six fixed milestones and an optional one: the second assessor. For each milestone you set its state (open, done or skipped) and, if you like, a hand-in date. The overview says which fixed milestone is next.

- **Start:** check the plan of approach with *Assess student work*. With *Keep the feedback in …* the feedback becomes a round in the track.
- **Visits:** prepare a visit with *Prepare a visit* (questions from the criteria) and write a report with *Report from notes*. The report goes to Word or the clipboard and is not kept. The track only remembers the date of the visit.
- **Drafts:** assess a draft with *Assess student work*, just like the final version. The track's earlier feedback goes along, and with *Keep the feedback in …* the assessment becomes a round in the track. With several drafts, do this per version.
- **Final version:** *Assess student work* gets the track's earlier feedback and says per criterion whether the requested improvement was taken up.
- **Second assessor (optional):** mostly for a graduation internship. Not counted in the progress. If you have one, fill in your level and your colleague's per criterion, with a rubric from Utilities. LimeOnIt shows the differences and what to discuss first.
- **Final interview:** an assessment at the end of the internship, often after a presentation by the student. How the final interview goes differs per programme: keep that once as *Setup of the final interview* with your assessment model. Prepare the interview with *Questions for the final interview*: questions per criterion, following that setup and, if you like, the student's presentation (as PDF, names out first), and what you still want to see before you judge. Write up your notes afterwards with *Report from notes*; that report is not kept. The criteria are filled in already.
- **Ready for OnStage:** see below.

## Ready for OnStage

For each criterion of the model you see the text that goes into OnStage. A first version comes from the last final assessment. Adjust it, choose **Confirm the texts**, and copy per criterion with **Copy**. Paste it into OnStage, check it and sign it there. LimeOnIt signs or sends nothing.

## Close

Once everything is in OnStage, choose **Close the track**. After that you cannot add anything. LimeOnIt keeps the track for a period for a possible appeal (90 days by default; the admin can change that) and then wipes it by itself. You see the date on which it will be wiped. Want to keep the whole feedback line yourself? Download it as Word; where you keep that file is your responsibility. With **Wipe now** in the list you wipe a track at once.

## Privacy

- **No name.** A track only has a code. A student's text first goes through the name check in your browser; only the anonymised text and the feedback are kept.
- **Yours alone.** A track is never shared, not with your team and not with a fellow supervisor.
- **Wiped.** A closed track is wiped after the retention period, with all its rounds.
- **Can be switched off.** The feature is on by default. An admin can switch it off under **Admin → Tools and models → Features**, for example on the privacy officer's advice.
