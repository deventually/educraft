# Making your BDB and BKE

This guide takes you step by step from an empty dossier to the documents you hand in. Allow a few working days if most of your practice material (lessons, feedback, a test you gave) is already there.

**Progress.** At the top of your dossier you see how far you are: first the framework, then each learning outcome on its own, then finishing. The *Next step* button takes you to what is up now; every step in the list is a link too, if you want to go deeper yourself. At the top of each learning outcome are four milestones: Evidence, Chapter, Assessment and Shown. So LU1 can be done while LU4 still waits for a test you gave. *Assessment* counts the coach's verdict, but also what you already marked as *Shown* yourself: the coach is a help, not a requirement.

**Tabs.** Your dossier has two tabs: Overview (your progress and your learning outcomes) and Finish (the check whether you can hand in, the export and your sources). Each learning outcome has four: Overview, Evidence, Chapter and Assessment. So you only see what you need now; the milestones and *Fix it* take you straight to the right tab.

**Three agreements first**

- **Your own institution's form is the standard.** Everything LimeOnIt says is measured against the criteria you take over and confirm.
- **You decide.** The coach advises; only you set a criterion to *Shown*.
- **You write yourself, unless you choose otherwise.** By default the coach only gives feedback. Having paragraphs drafted from your own evidence is possible, but it is a deliberate choice. Check what your institution says about AI use.

## Step 0: switch the workplace on

No **BDB/BKE** in the menu? Then the workplace is still off for you. It is *On request* by default, for admins too. An admin switches it on under **Admin → Tools and models → Features** (for everyone), through a team, or as an exception for one person.

**Done when:** you see **BDB/BKE** in the menu.

## Step 1: dossier and framework

1. Go to **BDB/BKE** and create a dossier. Give it a name and choose BDB, BKE or both. If you hand in each learning outcome on its own, one dossier is enough: you can export per learning outcome.
2. Open **Framework**. There are two ways:
   - **Take the framework from your form (recommended).** Paste the text of the assessment form, or choose the PDF or Word file. The file stays on your computer; only the text goes to the AI model. LimeOnIt sets out learning outcomes and criteria, and where the form names a "good" level, also *What makes it stronger*.
   - **Use the starting point.** The national starting point with the core tasks. If you don't have your form yet, you can confirm it as a *provisional framework*: the coach, drafting and the rehearsal assessment already work, and say that they judge on the national starting point. Replace it with your form before you hand in.
3. **Check every criterion.** A criterion that is not literally in the form gets *Check this*. Adjust it, or save it if it is right.
4. Under **Institution and form requirements**, fill in what your form asks: a maximum word count, the minimum number of test items you made or analysed (BKE), and whether an account of AI use is required. Add the **required evidence** per learning outcome, one per line.
5. Click **Confirm framework**. The coach, paragraph drafting and the rehearsal assessment judge against your criteria, so they need to be right.

**Done when:** the top of your dossier says "Framework confirmed on …".

## Step 2: collect what you already have

Open a learning outcome. Under **Add evidence**, paste text or take the text from a PDF or Word file. Choose the kind (lesson plan, observation, feedback, test material, analysis) and tick the criteria it belongs to. Don't have a piece yet? Add it without text: it is then *Still to come*.

Other evidence you already have:

- **My work:** anything you made in LimeOnIt before, with *Add to dossier*.
- **A course:** your test blueprint or coverage matrix, through *Use as evidence in BDB/BKE* on the course page. That is a snapshot; *Fetch again* updates it.
- **You may already have this:** at a criterion, LimeOnIt suggests earlier work that fits. Tick what you want to use.

**Done when:** every criterion has at least one linked piece of evidence.

## Step 3: fill gaps with a tool

When evidence is missing, LimeOnIt names at most two tools at that criterion that help. Examples:

| Criterion is about | Tool | What you fill in at least |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson preparation, lesson design | Prepare a lesson | learning goal and lesson content |
| Design from learning outcomes | Design a lesson or course | topic, big idea, learning outcomes |
| Test items, test blueprint (BKE) | Make test items and a blueprint | learning outcomes and subject matter |
| Assessment model, rubric (BKE) | Make an assessment model | learning outcome and assignment |
| Test analysis (BKE) | Explain a test analysis | the results of your analysis (p and Rit values) |
| Differences between students | Differentiate an assignment | learning goal and your assignment |
| Coherence in your teaching | Check a course for coherence | course outline and learning outcomes |

The required fields are your own material: without them the AI would make up your practice. After making it, click **Add to this criterion**; it is then evidence in your dossier.

Thinking aids for writing (Socratic partner, conversation with a theorist) help you think, but they are neither evidence nor a source.

**Done when:** no criterion is left without evidence.

## Step 4: write your chapters

You write one chapter per learning outcome. The word counter runs along, with your form's maximum, and counts the open spots still in your text.

- **Get a review.** The coach reads your chapter as a strict assessor and says per criterion *shown*, *partly* or *not yet*, with a literal quote from your text, what is missing and how to make it stronger. Quotes that are not really in your text and sources not in your list are filtered out. Save your chapter first.
- **Proofread.** Proposals for spelling, sentence structure, first person, repetition and APA, each with the sentence as it is now and a better version. *Take over* replaces exactly that sentence, *Ignore* removes the proposal.
- **Write part by part.** The Chapter tab lists the parts of your chapter: the outline from your form, or one per criterion when your form gives none. At a part, click *Ask me questions*. The coach asks two to four questions about your own practice, about what the criteria ask and your evidence does not show yet. Answer them briefly, in your own words; your answers are saved with your chapter. Nothing filled in yet and want other questions? Click *Other questions*.
- **Have paragraphs drafted (a choice).** Under *How do you want to use the coach?*, choose feedback and paragraphs. Each part with questions then has *Make a paragraph of it*: LimeOnIt writes that part from your answers and your linked evidence and puts it under the part's heading in your chapter. What is missing shows as `[to add: …]`; you fill in those spots yourself. If you choose feedback only, your answers stay beside your text and you write yourself.
- **Set the status.** Only you set a criterion to *In progress* or *Shown*. The coach never does.
- **References.** Put your sources in APA in the list. The check warns when you cite a source that is not in the list, or list a source you never use.

**Done when:** every chapter is written and you have set every criterion to *Shown*.

## Step 5: finish

1. **Ready to hand in?** The check at the bottom of your dossier marks each point green, orange or red, with *Fix* leading to where you fix it. You tick a few points yourself, such as "no student names in your evidence".
2. **Review the whole dossier.** All chapters at once, with an overview per learning outcome.
3. **Account of AI use.** If you used the coach or drafting, switch on the account at the export. If your institution requires it, the check says so.
4. **Download.** As Word or as PDF: a title page, a chapter per learning outcome, the account of AI use if you switch it on, the reference list (alphabetical) and your evidence as appendices.
5. **Practise your final interview.** With *Practise your final interview with a rehearsal assessment* you talk to a played committee that has read your dossier. After *Finish* you get feedback per criterion. It is practice: it never gives a grade.

**Done when:** the check is nowhere red and you have downloaded your documents.

## Open spots and the work you hand in

An open spot may be in your dossier while you work, but never in what you hand in. An open spot is:

- `[to add: …]` or `[fill in]` from a drafted paragraph, or TODO;
- an empty chapter;
- evidence without text (*Still to come*);
- a provisional framework.

While one is left, *Ready to hand in?* is red and the export only gives a **draft version**: DRAFT, DO NOT SUBMIT at the top and bottom of every page and on the title page, "(draft)" in the file name, and every open spot in yellow. Such a draft is handy to share with your workplace supervisor, or to keep working in Word.

## Hand in each learning outcome on its own

If you hand in each learning outcome separately, use *Word* or *PDF* at that learning outcome in the overview. You get a document of its own with a title page (code and title of the learning outcome), only that chapter, the sources it cites and the evidence for that outcome's criteria. Open spots count per learning outcome: LU1 can be final while LU3 is still a draft.

## On in Word, and back again

You may keep editing an export in Word and then carry on in LimeOnIt.

1. Download a draft or a final version as Word.
2. Work in Word. Keep the learning outcomes' headings (such as "LU1 Teaching", style Heading 1): that is how LimeOnIt recognises the chapters.
3. In the overview, choose your file under **Back from Word**. It is read in your browser and does not go to the server.
4. Per chapter you see whether it differs from LimeOnIt, with the word counts and the text from Word. Tick *Take over* for what you want to take over. You can tick new sources from your reference list too. Nothing is overwritten without your tick.

Open spots stay open spots. Your appendices are not read back; they stay as they are in LimeOnIt. Text under a heading that belongs to no learning outcome is not read; LimeOnIt names those headings, so you can move that text yourself. Headings, bold, italics, lists and tables come back; colours and fonts do not.

## Frequently asked questions

**The coach does nothing.** Confirm the framework first and save your chapter.

**"Answer a question or link evidence first."** Drafting a paragraph only works with your own answers or your own evidence as the source.

**Can I change the framework later?** Yes. After a change, confirm it again. Automatic extraction only works while no status, evidence or chapter depends on it; after that you adjust by hand.

**Does anyone else see my dossier?** No. A dossier is never shared, not even with your team. To let your workplace supervisor read along, send a draft version.
