# Teams and sharing

A **team** is a group of colleagues, for example your programme or department. An administrator creates teams and appoints team leads. Being a member shares nothing: everything stays private until you share something yourself.

You see your teams on **Account**, under *My teams*.

## What you can share

You can share three things with your team:

- a **course**, with the work you made with the tools for that course;
- a **rubric** from the utilities;
- a **teaching context**.

Choose **Share** on the course, rubric or teaching context, and then the team. Colleagues in the team can view it and choose **Make a copy** to make their own version. Only you edit the original. **Stop sharing** makes it private again; a copy a colleague already made stays theirs.

## What is never shared

- Work from tools that handle student work, such as feedback on a report.
- Conversations with a chat tool.
- Your BDB or BKE dossier and what you made for it.
- Filled-in rubrics with students' scores.

## What colleagues share with you

What colleagues share with your team is listed under **From my team**: on the Courses, Rubrics and Teaching context pages.

## For team leads

If you are a team lead, you open your team from **Account**. There you can:

- add a colleague by the email address they log in with;
- create an **invite link** for a new colleague, who then becomes a teacher and a member of your team;
- remove a colleague from the team, or make them team lead too;
- stop being team lead yourself.

Removing another team lead is done by an administrator.
