# Teams and sharing

A **team** is a group of colleagues, for example your programme or department. An administrator creates teams and appoints team leads. Being a member shares nothing: everything stays private until you share something yourself.

You see your teams under **Education › Teams**. Open a team to see who is in it, who leads it and which programmes the team works on. As a plain member the page is read only; email addresses and the team's choices are for the team lead. What you share with your team, all these colleagues see.

## What you can share

You can share three things with your team:

- a **course**, with the work you made with the tools for that course;
- a **rubric** from the utilities;
- a **teaching context**.

Choose **Share** on the course, rubric or teaching context, and then the team. Colleagues in the team can view it and choose **Make a copy** to make their own version. Only you (and, for a course, whoever works on it) edit the original. **Stop sharing** makes it private again; a copy a colleague already made stays theirs.

## Working on a course together

Sharing only lets colleagues read. To work on a course together, click **Add a colleague** on the course, under *Work on this course together*. You tick one or more colleagues you share a team with, and you choose a role:

- An **editor** sees the course under **Courses**, with the owner's name on it, and changes outcomes, activities, assessments and settings.
- A **co-owner** can do that too, and can also share the course with their own team and add or remove co-owners and editors. That way a module team can run a course together.
- Only the **owner** deletes the course, chooses its educational context and hands it over.

If a whole team works on it, such as a module team, click **Add team** and tick the team. Everyone in that team works on it with the role you choose, also whoever joins the team later. You change the role in the row, and **Remove** always asks first.

What someone makes with a tool and links to the course stays theirs. So do the review team's reviews. Anyone working on it can **Stop working on it** themselves.

Changes are saved per part. If you work on different parts at the same time, everything is kept. If two people change the same part, the last save counts.

### Handing a course over

Leaving, or is a colleague taking the course on? Choose **Hand the course over** at the bottom of the course. You pick a colleague from your teams or someone who already works on it. That colleague is the owner at once. Shared teams, the place in programmes and who works on it go along; the educational context does not, the new owner chooses it. By default you keep working on it as editor. Untick that if you want to let go of the course completely.

If a teacher drops out, the admin hands their courses over on that teacher's page, under **Courses**. That also works when the account is no longer active. When an account is deleted, a course with a co-owner goes by itself to the co-owner who has worked on it longest. A course without a co-owner goes with the account; the delete screen warns about it.

## What is never shared

- Work from tools that handle student work, such as feedback on a report.
- Conversations with a chat tool.
- Your BDB or BKE dossier and what you made for it.
- Filled-in rubrics with students' scores.

## What colleagues share with you

What colleagues share with your team is listed under **From my team**: on the Courses, Rubrics and Teaching context pages.

## For team leads

If you are a team lead, you open your team under **Education › Teams**. There you can:

- add colleagues with **Add members**: search by name or email address, tick who should join and choose **Add**. You see every teacher with an active account who is not in your team yet; admins are not listed;
- create an **invite link** for a new colleague, who then becomes a teacher and a member of your team;
- remove anyone from the team with **Remove**, also another team lead (it asks first), or make a colleague team lead too;
- link your team to programmes with **Add programme**, or take it off with **Remove**;
- choose **this team's default model**: your members' tools start on it, unless a teacher has a default of their own. You choose from the models the institution has on;
- stop being team lead yourself.

You do not remove yourself; for that, choose **Stop being team lead**.

## For administrators

An administrator sees every team under **Education › Teams**, searches by team or person and creates a team with **New team**. On the same team page as the lead, the administrator has more buttons: rename the team, choose where it belongs, tick the team leads, remove any member and delete the team.

Under **Where does this team belong?** the administrator picks one of three: *to programmes* (the team belongs where its programmes belong), *to a faculty* (for a team without a programme, such as a department) or *to the whole institution* (such as an exam board). A team linked to programmes belongs to them. To have it belong to a faculty or the whole institution, first remove the programmes under **Programmes**; that way nothing disappears unnoticed.
