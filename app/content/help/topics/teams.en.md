# Teams and sharing

A **team** is a group of colleagues, for example your programme or department. An administrator creates teams and appoints team leads. Being a member shares nothing: everything stays private until you share something yourself.

You see your teams on **Account**, under *My teams*.

## What you can share

You can share three things with your team:

- a **course**, with the work you made with the tools for that course;
- a **rubric** from the utilities;
- a **teaching context**.

Choose **Share** on the course, rubric or teaching context, and then the team. Colleagues in the team can view it and choose **Make a copy** to make their own version. Only you (and, for a course, your editors) edit the original. **Stop sharing** makes it private again; a copy a colleague already made stays theirs.

## Working on a course together

Sharing only lets colleagues read. To work on a course together, add a colleague as **editor**. You do that at the bottom of the course, under *Work on this course together*. You can choose colleagues you share a team with.

- An editor sees the course under **Courses**, with your name on it, and changes outcomes, activities, assessments and settings as you do.
- What an editor makes with a tool and links to the course stays theirs. So do the review team's reviews.
- Only you as the owner delete the course, share it with a team, choose its educational context and choose editors.
- An editor can **Stop editing** themselves; you can remove an editor.

Changes are saved per part. If you work on different parts at the same time, everything is kept. If two people change the same part, the last save counts.

## What is never shared

- Work from tools that handle student work, such as feedback on a report.
- Conversations with a chat tool.
- Your BDB or BKE dossier and what you made for it.
- Filled-in rubrics with students' scores.

## What colleagues share with you

What colleagues share with your team is listed under **From my team**: on the Courses, Rubrics and Teaching context pages.

## For team leads

If you are a team lead, you open your team from **Account**. There you can:

- add a colleague by the email address they log in with;
- create an **invite link** for a new colleague, who then becomes a teacher and a member of your team;
- remove a colleague from the team, or make them team lead too;
- stop being team lead yourself.

Removing another team lead is done by an administrator.
