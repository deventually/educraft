# Utilities without AI

Utilities only calculate or organise. They use no AI: they are fast, free and always give the same answer. You find them under **Tools**, below the AI tools, and through the search bar.

## Converting points to a grade

Fill in the maximum number of points and the pass mark, and you get a grade table. For a multiple-choice test you can switch on a **guessing correction**: the score you get on average by guessing then counts as the lowest grade. Under *More settings* you adjust the lowest and highest grade and the number of decimals. Always check that the outcome matches your programme's exam regulations.

## Making and keeping rubrics

Put criteria and levels in a rubric and save it. Then you fill it in per student, and turn the choices into a grade. Students' names stay in your browser and are not saved.

You can share a rubric with your team. Colleagues can then view it and make their own copy. See [Teams and sharing](/help/teams).

## Analysing test results per question

Paste the scores after a test, for example from Excel. Per question you see the **p-value** (how easy), the **Rit** and **Rir** (how well the question discriminates), and for the whole test **Cronbach's alpha** (how reliable). Names are removed first.

Want to know what the numbers mean? Choose **Explain**. That opens an AI tool with only the numbers per question filled in; the score list does not go to the AI.

Don't see any utilities? Then they are not (yet) switched on for your team.
