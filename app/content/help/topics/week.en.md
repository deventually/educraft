# Your week

At the top of **Start** you see what happens this week across all your courses. LimeOnIt works it out from what you fill in yourself. No AI is involved, and nothing runs in the background.

## Setting a start date

Open your course and fill in the **start date** under *Course details*. Week 1 is the week that holds that date. Every lesson and assessment then shows its dates too, for example "Week 3 · Nov 17 – 23". LimeOnIt skips your school's break weeks: the administrator enters them once for the whole school.

## What you see on Start

For every course that is running now:

- **In class:** this week's lessons, assignments and assessments.
- **To prepare:** what is on this week and next week but has no work linked to it yet. Link a lesson plan or a test to that item and it leaves the list.
- **To grade:** the assessments of the two previous weeks.
- **Points of attention:** how many checks are open, and the reviewers' advice you have not decided on yet.

In a break week you only see "Break". From **Friday to Sunday** there is also a **Next week** block, so you can go into the weekend with an overview.

## In your calendar

A course with a start date shows **Add to your calendar**. You download a calendar file (.ics) with your lessons and assessments per week. Open it in Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar. Importing it again later updates the events instead of doubling them. The file only holds what you typed: the course name and the names of your lessons and assessments.
