# Your account

Manage your own account under **Account**: change the email address you log in with, or
set a new password. Both changes ask for your current password first.

## Which AI models you can use

You don't pick the AI model yourself — your teacher (and the administrator) decide which
models are available, including local and CLI models. In a tutor set up for your cohort the
model is fixed for you, so you can focus on the conversation.

## Removing your account (students)

If you ask to remove your account, it is **disabled** rather than immediately deleted — your
teacher may still need your work for grading. You are logged out and cannot log back in.

- Your teacher is notified on their cohort screen.
- They can **restore** your access, or **permanently remove** your account and all your data.

If you disabled your account by mistake, ask your teacher to restore it.

## Handling removal requests (teachers & admins)

Open the cohort under **Cohorts**. A student who asked to leave is shown in the **Students**
section with a *removal requested* badge (and the cohort list shows a count). For each student
you can:

- **Restore access** — re-enable the account so the student can log in again.
- **Remove student** — permanently delete their account, chat history and cohort membership.
  This cannot be undone, so it asks you to confirm.

You can only manage students in cohorts you own or co-teach; administrators can manage any
cohort.
