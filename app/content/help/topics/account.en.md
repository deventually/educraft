# Your account

Manage your own account under **Account**: change the email address you log in with, or
set a new password. Both changes ask for your current password first.

You can be signed in on several devices at once (for example a laptop and a phone). When you
set a new password, every other device is signed out; the device you changed it on stays
signed in.

## Which AI models you can use

The administrator decides which AI models are available to you. You pick from that set in the
model menu of each tool.

## Deleting your account

Under **Account** you can delete your account yourself. Type the confirmation word and your
account is removed immediately, together with your saved work, context profiles and usage
data. This cannot be undone.
