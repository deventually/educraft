# Your account

You find your account in the menu at the top right, under **Account**.

## Email address and password

You can change the email address you log in with, and set a new password (at least 10 characters). Both first ask for your current password.

If you set a new password, you are logged out on all your other devices. The device on which you change it stays logged in.

## Two-step verification

With two-step verification, LimeOnIt asks for a six-digit code from an app on your phone after your password. Someone who only knows your password can't get in.

1. On **Account**, choose *Set up two-step verification*.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, such as Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or the Passwords app on an iPhone.
3. Type the code the app shows.
4. Keep the **recovery codes** somewhere safe. If you lose your phone, you log in with one of those codes. Each code works once.

If you run out of recovery codes, make new ones. Two-step verification is required for administrators.

## Logging out on all your devices

After 8 hours without activity you are logged out automatically. Still logged in somewhere, for example on a computer at school? Then choose **Log out everywhere** on **Account**. You are logged out on all your devices at once, including the one you are on now.

## Language

At the top of every page you switch between Dutch and English. The tools write their result in the language you pick under *Output language*.

## Which AI models you can use

Your administrator decides which AI models are available to you. You choose from that set under **Advanced** on the tool page.

## Your teams

If you are in a team, it shows on **Account**. Being a member shares none of your work. See [Teams and sharing](/help/teams).

## Deleting your account

Under **Account** you can delete your account yourself. Type the confirmation word and your account is deleted immediately, together with your saved work, teaching contexts and usage data. This cannot be undone.
