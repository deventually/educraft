# Programmes

A **programme** is a bundle of the courses of one or more teams. Each course shows the **year** and the **period** it falls in within that programme. That way you see the programme as a whole, not just course by course.

## Of one or more teams

A programme belongs to one or more **teams**, such as the programme team and the module teams that make its courses. You can be in several teams; you then see the programmes of all of them. Each programme shows its teams.

## Where to find them

In the **Education** menu, click **Programmes**. The list is per faculty, with the programmes without a faculty last. A programme has three tabs: **Courses**, **Learning lines** and **Details** (name, level, mode, faculty and teams). On top is the way back, such as *Programmes › Computer Science full-time*.

- Only an **admin** creates and manages a programme: name, level, mode, learning lines and deleting.
- **Every member** of one of those teams can put their own courses in it, and courses they co-own, and sees their colleagues' courses in the programme.
- An **admin** links any team to one or more programmes, a **team lead** only their own team. On the programme's page, click **Add team** under **Teams**; on a team's page, click **Add programme**. In the window you search and tick one or more. **Remove** always asks first; the team and the programme both stay. Only an admin sees a programme without a team.

## Create a programme

The admin does this. Go to **Admin → Faculties and programmes** and click **New programme** in the faculty. If your institution has no faculties, click **New programme** under **Programmes** in the **Education** menu. Fill in:

- **Team**: the first team, or **No team yet**; you link the teams later.
- **Name**, for example *Computer Science full-time* or *Master Software Engineering*.
- **Type and level**, for example bachelor (hbo), associate degree (Ad), master (hbo) or wo master. You choose from the types your administrator has switched on.
- **Mode**: full-time, part-time, work-based (dual) or **minor**. This may stay empty.

## Add courses

On the programme's page, click **Add course**. In the window, tick one or more of your own courses, or courses you co-own, and choose the year and period. The year runs from 1 to 8 and the period from 1 to 6. Both may stay empty; such a course comes last.

You can also do this from the course. The course page has **Belongs to programme**, where you choose the programme, the year and the period.

**Putting a course in a programme shares it with your teams of that programme.** Every colleague in the programme sees it there; your colleagues in those teams can view and copy it, just as with [sharing with your team](/help/teams). Only you and whoever works on the course edit it. You change the year and period of your own course yourself.

A course can belong to **several programmes**, with its own year and period in each. Think of a full-time and a part-time variant with the same courses, or a course that falls in year 1 of one programme and year 2 of another.

## Learning lines

A **learning line** is a series of courses within a programme, in order, for example *Programming* or *Research*. The admin creates it on the programme's page: give it a name and tick the courses. On the learning line's page you put the courses in order with **Up** and **Down**, and add or remove one. Every team member can view the learning line. All learning lines of your programmes are under **Education → Learning lines**.

The learning line shows **what comes back**: a learning outcome that occurs in several courses, with the highest Bloom level in each course. An outcome comes back when a later course has the same text (capitals, spaces and a closing full stop don't count), or the same **subject tag**, such as the same professional task from your teaching context. If the Bloom level drops in a later course, it says: *The Bloom level drops*. Below that are the learning outcomes per course.

Like the coverage matrix, this is **advice**. Nothing changes in the courses, and no AI is involved. To follow a thread, use the same wording or the same tag in each course.

## What goes where?

- A **master's** is a programme of its own.
- A **minor** is a programme of its own with the mode *minor*.
- A **part-time** or **work-based** variant is a programme of its own, with the same courses in it.

## Good to know

- The owner or the admin may take a course out of a programme (**Remove**, after a confirmation). The course stays as it is, and stays shared with the team. You can stop sharing on the course page.
- If you stop sharing with the team, or leave the team, your course leaves that team's programmes, unless you are also in another team of the programme and share it with that one.
- If a team is deleted, its programmes stay. A programme left without a team is seen only by the admin, until a team joins again.
- If you delete a course, it disappears from all programmes.
- Only an **admin** deletes a programme, with **Delete programme** at the bottom of the programme's page. All courses stay as they are.
- No AI is involved: a programme only records what you fill in yourself.
