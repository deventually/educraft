# Earlier work: find it and export it

Everything you make with the tools is saved automatically under **Earlier work**. You get there through the link on **Start** and **Tools**, and at the bottom of every page.

## Finding it again

The newest is at the top, with the tool's name and the date. Open a result to read it again or copy it.

## Exporting to Word or PDF

Choose **Export** on a result. You first see a preview of the file. Then you choose:

- **Word (.docx)**, to carry on writing in Word;
- **Markdown (.md)**, for those who work with plain text;
- **Print or save as PDF**: choose *Save as PDF* in the print window.

## Linking to a course

You can link work to a course: to the whole course, or to one lesson or test in it. The course page then lists it under **Linked work**, and on **Start** that lesson drops off *To prepare*. You can also link straight from the tool, by choosing your course there. See [Courses and the alignment spine](/help/courses).

## Deleting

**Delete** removes a result for good. Its link to a course goes too.

## What nobody else sees

Your work is yours. Colleagues only see it if you share a course with your team, and then never work about students and never a conversation. See [Teams and sharing](/help/teams).
