# Getting started

LimeOnIt turns evidence-based teaching methods from *The Pedagogical Promptbook* into tools for teachers in secondary, vocational and higher education. You fill in what you need, and the AI drafts a first version that fits your lesson. You decide what to do with it.

## In four steps

1. **Say where you teach.** Set up your [teaching context](/help/context-profiles) once: type of education, programme or subject, level. Every tool takes it into account.
2. **Pick a tool.** Under **Tools** they are grouped by goal, such as *Plan & design* or *Assess & give feedback*. On **Start** you can simply type what you want to do, for example *write test questions*.
3. **Fill in and generate.** Some tools make a result in one go, others hold a conversation with you. See [Working with a tool](/help/using-tools).
4. **Keep it and carry on.** Everything you make is in [Earlier work](/help/saved-work). From there you export it to Word or PDF.

## What else there is

- **Courses:** record your learning outcomes, lessons and tests and see whether they line up. Tools then fill in your learning outcomes for you. See [Courses and the alignment spine](/help/courses).
- **Your week:** on **Start** you see what happens in your courses this week. See [Your week](/help/week).
- **Utilities:** calculations without AI, such as turning points into a grade. See [Utilities](/help/utilities).
- **Teams:** share a course, rubric or teaching context with colleagues. See [Teams and sharing](/help/teams).
- **BDB and BKE:** work on your Dutch teaching qualification. See [Making your BDB and BKE](/help/bdb-bke).

Not everything is on for everyone. If you don't see something, your administrator has not switched it on (yet).

## Tips

- **A concrete context gives a more useful result.** Mention the professional practice and the framework of your subject.
- **The result is a starting point.** Read it and adapt it before you use it with students.
- **Look under the hood.** On every tool page, *Rationale & source* shows where the method comes from, and *View original prompt* shows the book's text.
- **Help for a screen.** If a screen shows *How does this work?* under its title, it takes you to the help for that screen.
