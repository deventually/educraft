Research report, advisory report or report — general starter model. Adapt the criteria to the assignment and the level of your own programme.

C1 — Question and purpose.
- Sufficient: the purpose and the research or advisory question are clearly formulated and delimited.
- Good: the question is sharp, relevant for the client and logically divided into sub-questions.

C2 — Support from sources.
- Sufficient: relevant and reliable sources are used and referenced correctly.
- Good: sources are compared and weighed critically.

C3 — Approach and method.
- Sufficient: the approach fits the question and is described so it can be followed.
- Good: the choices are justified and limitations are named.

C4 — Results and analysis.
- Sufficient: the findings are presented factually and clearly, and analysed.
- Good: the analysis is in depth and connects the findings.

C5 — Conclusion and recommendations.
- Sufficient: the conclusion answers the question based on the findings; recommendations follow from it.
- Good: recommendations are concrete, feasible and substantiated for the audience.

C6 — Structure and language.
- Sufficient: logical structure, businesslike and careful language that suits the reader.
- Good: clear, concise and convincing; virtually error-free.
