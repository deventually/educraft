Graduation thesis / final project report (higher professional education) — general starter model. Adapt the criteria and levels to your own programme's exit qualifications.

C1 — Problem statement and relevance.
- Sufficient: the background, the problem and the main question are clear and well delimited; the relevance for the client or the professional field is substantiated.
- Good: the problem statement is sharp and original, and its relevance for both practice and the discipline is convincingly shown.

C2 — Theoretical framework.
- Sufficient: relevant concepts and theories are worked out with reliable sources and used in the research.
- Good: the framework is built critically and coherently from (scholarly) sources and visibly steers the research choices.

C3 — Method and justification.
- Sufficient: research design, data collection and analysis fit the sub-questions and are described so they can be followed; reliability, validity and ethics are addressed.
- Good: the choices are weighed critically against alternatives and limitations are named honestly.

C4 — Results and analysis.
- Sufficient: the results are presented clearly and factually and answer the sub-questions.
- Good: the analysis is in depth, links results to the theoretical framework and also shows contradictory findings.

C5 — Conclusion, discussion and recommendations.
- Sufficient: the conclusion answers the main question based on the results; recommendations are concrete and follow from the conclusion.
- Good: the discussion places the findings critically in context and the recommendations are directly usable for the client.

C6 — Work product or advice (if applicable).
- Sufficient: the product or advice follows from the research findings and is usable for the client.
- Good: the product is of professional quality and tested with its intended users.

C7 — Writing and use of sources.
- Sufficient: logical structure, businesslike and careful language, consistent referencing (e.g. APA).
- Good: clear and convincing for the audience; virtually error-free.

C8 — Reflection on own performance.
- Sufficient: the student reflects on process, choices and own development with concrete examples.
- Good: the reflection is critical, linked to the exit qualifications and leads to concrete learning intentions.
