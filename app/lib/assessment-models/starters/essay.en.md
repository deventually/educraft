Essay — general starter model. Adapt the criteria to the assignment and the level of your own programme.

C1 — Thesis or central question.
- Sufficient: the essay has a clear thesis or central question that gives direction to the whole piece.
- Good: the thesis is sharp, thought-provoking and well delimited.

C2 — Argumentation.
- Sufficient: the arguments support the thesis and are built up logically.
- Good: the argumentation is convincing, weighs counter-arguments and refutes them.

C3 — Use of sources.
- Sufficient: relevant sources support the arguments and are referenced correctly.
- Good: sources are used critically and related to each other.

C4 — Structure.
- Sufficient: introduction, body and conclusion are recognisable; paragraphs follow on logically.
- Good: the structure strengthens the argument; the ending draws a clear conclusion.

C5 — Language and style.
- Sufficient: correct, careful language that suits the genre.
- Good: an own, clear voice; virtually error-free.
