# Rubric — Guided Reflection (Backward Design)

Judge the output as a Backward-Design lesson plan for a teacher. Score each shared
criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it work backward (desired results → acceptable evidence →
  learning activities) and use the supplied big idea, essential question, outcomes
  and evidence rather than ignoring them?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Is the assessment evidence genuinely aligned to the
  stated learning outcomes (Understanding by Design)? No invented outcomes,
  standards, or sources.
- **formatAdherence** — Clear stages/sections a teacher can act on.
- **levelFit** — Register and cognitive demand fit the case's EQF level (the EQF 3
  case should be markedly more concrete than the EQF 6 case).
- **languageQuality** — Correct, professional Dutch; no anglicisms.

Penalise fabricated standards/outcomes, misalignment between evidence and outcomes,
or a plan that ignores the provided inputs.
