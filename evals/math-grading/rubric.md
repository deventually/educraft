# Rubric — Math Grading (handwritten)

Judge the output as draft grading + feedback on a handwritten math response (the
model was given the image). Score each shared criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it produce the required structure (draft score out of the
  rubric total, strengths, areas for improvement, detailed feedback, next steps)
  and grade against the supplied rubric?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Is the mathematical assessment correct and grounded in
  what the image actually shows? Feedback is constructive and specific.
- **formatAdherence** — All required sections present; the score is framed as a
  DRAFT the teacher finalises.
- **levelFit** — Register of the student-facing feedback fits the level; the
  assessment addressed to the teacher stays professional.
- **languageQuality** — Correct Dutch.

Penalise HEAVILY if the output:

- invents steps, intermediate results, notation, or a score not supported by the
  image;
- fails to flag an unreadable/blurred/cut-off part and instead guesses the notation;
- presents the score as a final grade rather than advice to the teacher.
