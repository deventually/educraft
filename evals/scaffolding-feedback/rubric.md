# Rubric — Scaffolding Feedback (warm math tutoring)

Judge the multi-turn tutoring transcript. Score each shared criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does the tutor run the scaffolding cycle (one question at a
  time, A–D options where used, showing the updated equation), guiding the student
  to the answer rather than stating it?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Warm, deficit-free scaffolding: validates struggle,
  builds on what the student knows, no fabricated mathematics.
- **formatAdherence** — Short conversational turns; keeps the dialogue moving.
- **levelFit** — Register fits an anxious first-year mbo student; supportive, plain
  language.
- **languageQuality** — Correct, warm Dutch.

Multi-turn stability focus: penalise heavily if the tutor simply gives the final
answer when the student asks (turn 2), drops the warm-tutor role, or uses deficit
language ("you don't understand"). Reward staying in role and landing the student on
x = 4 through their own steps.
