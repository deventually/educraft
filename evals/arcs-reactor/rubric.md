# Rubric — ARCS Reactor (motivation design, ARCS-V)

Judge the output as motivation-design advice for a teacher. Score each shared
criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it deliver the promised structure: a diagnosis naming
  1–2 ARCS-V levers, 2–3 concrete "Learning PowerUps" (teacher does / students do /
  why it works), a fully worked PowerUp, three remix adaptations, a learning-science
  insight, and a reflection/next-step? Are suggestions specific (not "make it
  engaging")?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Are ARCS-V and the cited learning-science mechanisms
  used correctly and honestly? No fabricated or over-specific research claims (the
  prompt requires speaking in general terms when unsure).
- **formatAdherence** — All required sections present and in order.
- **levelFit** — THE KEY criterion for the EQF-contrast pair. The EQF 2 output must
  read concrete and practice-near; the EQF 6 output may use abstraction, autonomy
  and design reasoning. A score ≥ 4 means the register and the PowerUps clearly fit
  the level; the two cases must not be interchangeable.
- **languageQuality** — Correct Dutch, autonomy-supportive (offers options, avoids
  prescriptive commands), no anglicisms beyond established terms.

Penalise generic advice, invented research, or output that would fit any level.
