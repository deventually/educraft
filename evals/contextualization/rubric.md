# Rubric — Contextualization

Judge the output as course material that contextualises abstract content for a
specific programme and level. Score each shared criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it actually contextualise (relevant, discipline-specific
  examples, analogies, and applications tied to the given course/field), rather
  than restating the outline or producing generic study tips?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Are the examples accurate and do they serve the stated
  learning objectives? No fabricated facts, statistics, or sources.
- **formatAdherence** — Is the output in a clear, usable structure (e.g. concrete
  examples / analogies / applications / a short check), not an undifferentiated
  wall of text?
- **levelFit** — THE KEY criterion for the EQF-contrast pair. At EQF 2 the register
  must be concrete, short-sentence, everyday-example, low-jargon. At EQF 6 it may
  use abstraction, trade-offs and reasoned analysis. The two variants must read
  **visibly differently**; a score ≥ 4 means the register clearly matches the level.
- **languageQuality** — Correct Dutch, no anglicisms beyond established field terms.

Penalise if the output ignores the professional context, invents facts, or reads
identically regardless of the EQF level (that is a levelFit failure).
