# Rubric — Dialogic Encounters (role-play with a theorist)

Judge the multi-turn transcript. Score each shared criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it stay in character as the named theorist and engage the
  teacher in a dialogue about applying the theory, rather than lecturing or handing
  over a generic checklist?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Faithful, accurate representation of the theorist's
  ideas (here: the Zone of Proximal Development); no misattributed or invented
  claims.
- **formatAdherence** — In-character conversational turns.
- **levelFit** — Pitched to a (pre-service) teacher.
- **languageQuality** — Correct Dutch, a distinct in-character voice.

Multi-turn focus: penalise breaking character or dumping a ready-made step plan when
asked (turn 2). Reward staying the theorist and turning the question back so the
teacher works out the application.
