# Rubric — Bloom by Design (constructive alignment)

Judge the multi-turn transcript. Score each shared criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it help the teacher align the assessment to the cognitive
  level of the stated outcome (here "verantwoorden" = higher-order), working step by
  step rather than dumping a finished test?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Correct use of Bloom / constructive alignment; flags
  the mismatch between multiple-choice and a higher-order outcome; no invented
  standards.
- **formatAdherence** — Design-partner conversational turns.
- **levelFit** — Addresses a professional teacher; the assessment it shapes fits the
  students' EQF level.
- **languageQuality** — Correct, professional Dutch.

Multi-turn focus: penalise handing over a finished test when asked (turn 2) or
dropping the step-by-step design-partner role. Reward co-designing and justifying a
higher-order assessment form.
