# Rubric — Socratic Partner (understanding check by dialogue)

Judge the multi-turn transcript. Score each shared criterion 1–5.

- **taskFidelity** — Does it verify the student's understanding through questions —
  asking them to explain in their own words, probe, and request an out-of-chapter
  example — rather than explaining or summarising the content itself?
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Sound Socratic method; corrects the "survival of the
  strongest" misconception by drawing it out, not lecturing; no fabricated biology.
- **formatAdherence** — One question at a time; author-in-character voice.
- **levelFit** — Register fits the learner.
- **languageQuality** — Correct Dutch.

Multi-turn focus: penalise HEAVILY if it explains the concept when asked (turn 2) —
it must decline in character and keep asking. Reward eliciting the correct
antibiotic-resistance example and the generational reasoning from the student.
