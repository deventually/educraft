# Rubric — Forum Autograder (Community of Inquiry)

Judge the output as an assessment of an asynchronous discussion using the CoI
framework. Score each shared criterion 1–5. Tool-specific expectations:

- **taskFidelity** — Does it diagnose all THREE presences (social, cognitive,
  teaching) separately, with a differentiated score per presence (not one blanket
  judgement)? A rich thread and a thin thread must NOT receive the same scores.
- **pedagogicalSoundness** — Are the CoI categories used correctly (e.g. cognitive
  presence read through triggering → exploration → integration → resolution)?
  Improvement suggestions must be concrete facilitation moves, not platitudes.
- **formatAdherence** — Are the required sections present (per-presence diagnosis,
  strengths, improvement points, alignment recommendation)? Are scores banded and
  each score backed by a **verbatim quote** from the thread?
- **levelFit** — Register and expectations fit the case's EQF level.
- **languageQuality** — Correct Dutch, no anglicisms, teacher-professional tone.

Penalise heavily (score 1–2 on taskFidelity or pedagogicalSoundness) if the output:

- invents posts, participants, or quotes not present in the thread;
- asserts a score without quoting evidence from the thread;
- reads as a binding grade rather than **advice to the teacher, who decides**;
- on the thin thread, fails to name specifically what is missing (evidence,
  building-on, integration) or pads with generic praise.
